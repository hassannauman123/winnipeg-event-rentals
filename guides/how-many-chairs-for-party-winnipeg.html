How Many Chairs Do You Need for a Winnipeg Party?

A quick seating guide for backyard parties, BBQs, birthdays, graduations, showers, and family events.

Use your guest count as the starting point

For most parties, rent enough chairs for your expected guest count plus a small buffer. Even casual events need more seating than people expect because guests eat, rest, visit with family, and move between areas.

Event size Suggested chair count Best use
20 guests 22 to 25 chairs Small birthday or BBQ
40 guests 44 to 50 chairs Backyard party or shower
75 guests 80 to 90 chairs Graduation or family event
100 guests 100 chairs Large local gathering

Plan for eating areas and visiting areas

If guests will eat at tables, keep chairs near the food area. If the event is casual, place extra chairs in shaded spots, near the entrance, or around the yard so older guests and families can sit comfortably.

Pickup or delivery

Pickup is a good option for smaller chair rentals. For larger Winnipeg parties, delivery and setup can save time and reduce the number of trips needed before and after the event.
