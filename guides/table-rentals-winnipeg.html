Table Rentals in Winnipeg for Parties, Weddings, and Backyard Events

6 ft folding tables are useful for food service, gifts, displays, sign-in areas, graduation parties, vendor tables, and family gatherings.

What can you use a 6 ft folding table for?

A 6 ft folding table is one of the most flexible event rental items. It can work as a food table, dessert table, gift table, registration table, display table, game table, or extra surface for a backyard BBQ.

How many tables should you rent?

For small Winnipeg events, one or two tables may be enough for food and gifts. Larger backyard parties, graduations, and community events may need separate tables for serving, drinks, desserts, and displays.
